Designing a reading surface

A good reader disappears. This paragraph has bold, italic, struck text, inline code, a remote link, a jump link, and “curly quotes” that should look right. Press ⌘F to find. Inline math like E = mc^2 sits in the line without shifting the baseline, and so does \sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}.

Typography

Weight carries hierarchy as much as size. Headings stay in the same family as the body, track slightly tight, and keep generous space above so that sections breathe. Long lines are capped near 68 characters so the eye never has to travel far; this sentence runs on a little to make sure that the wrapping behaviour and the line-height are easy to judge by eye.
